What This Is

Engineering that solves real problems

The Daddy G.O Innovation Challenge is an annual engineering competition hosted by NUESA UNIBEN. Now in its 4th edition, this year introduces a mandatory Design Phase before any hardware is built — because a device that nobody needed is not an innovation.

Open to all UNIBEN students across all faculties. Mixed-faculty teams are explicitly encouraged and separately recognised. If you understand the problem, we want you here.

How The Innovation Challenge Works

Three phases from idea to Grand Finale

No team builds anything before their problem has been evaluated. That's the rule. That's why this edition is different.

1
June 1 – June 14, 2026
Design Phase Submission

Prove the problem. Plan the solution. Pass the innovation gate. Every team submits a written design document before any hardware is built. Judges evaluate your proposal blindly — they don't know your name, only your idea.

  • Problem Statement — with real numbers, real people, real harm
  • Solution Overview — what the device does, exactly
  • Hardware Justification — not a shopping list, a reasoned component plan
  • Software/Logic Design — a flowchart that shows what happens when things go wrong
  • Preliminary Component Budget — sourced from Nigerian suppliers
  • Innovation Statement — why this needs to exist
2
June 23 – July 2, 2026
Build & Mentorship Week

Shortlisted teams are assigned mentors based on their specific design — not generic tutorials. You build your prototype, document the process, and prepare for the final presentation. Mentors help. You build.

  • Access to workshop facilities and assigned technical mentor
  • Working prototype (or functional partial prototype with documentation)
  • Build documentation — what changed from your design, and why
  • 2–3 minute video demonstration submitted by July 2nd
3
July 10, 2026 · Grand Finale
Demonstrate. Defend. Win.

Top teams present live to a panel of judges drawn equally from academia and industry. This is not a sales pitch — it is an engineering defence. You explain your design decisions, what failed, what you changed, and what measurable improvement your device achieves.

  • 8-minute live demonstration and presentation
  • 7-minute judges' Q&A — defend every decision
  • Judges' deliberation and scoring
  • Award ceremony — ₦1,000,000 first prize

Seeded Problem Directions

Need a starting point?

These are examples of the calibre of problem this challenge is looking for. Teams are not required to choose from this list — but every submission must meet this standard.

Health & Safety
Generator CO Poisoning Interceptor

Carbon monoxide from indoor generators kills hundreds of Nigerians annually. Cheap CO detectors beep — but victims lose consciousness before they can act.

Core challenge: Reliable relay cutoff on generator ignition + GSM alert within a battery-backed enclosure that operates when mains power is out.
Water & Environment
Flood Early Warning Network

Benin City floods annually with no localised warning infrastructure. Residents receive no advance notice before water enters their homes.

Core challenge: Multi-node LoRa mesh with ultrasonic sensors at drainage chokepoints. No internet required. Solar-powered with 72-hour battery backup.
Commerce & Trade
Adulterated Fuel Detector

Adulterated petrol destroys generators and engines. Industrial testers cost tens of thousands of dollars. No affordable point-of-purchase option exists in Nigeria.

Core challenge: Handheld device using capacitive or optical sensing of fuel dielectric properties. Pass/fail result before you refuel.
Agriculture & Food Security
Post-Harvest Cold Shelf

Market traders selling perishables have no access to cold storage. End-of-day loss selling is the norm — not the exception.

Core challenge: PCM selection and thermal architecture — must maintain target temperature for 8–10 hours with zero active power draw during market hours.
Health & Safety
Anti-Sleep Alert for Drivers

Commercial driver fatigue is a leading cause of mass-casualty accidents on Nigerian highways. No affordable embedded intervention currently exists.

Core challenge: Infrared eye-tracking + head-position gyroscope, triggering haptic and audible alarms. Self-calibrating to the individual driver's baseline in darkness and vibration.
Water & Environment
Borehole Water Quality Guardian

Communities rely on boreholes never tested after installation. Contamination causes chronic illness rarely traced back to the water source.

Core challenge: Continuous pH, turbidity, conductivity, and temperature monitoring. Waterproof enclosure, long-term unattended operation, SMS reports to community leadership.
Health & Safety · Commerce & Trade
Smart Market Fire Alert Mesh

Market fires in Nigeria are recurrent. Poor communication means fire spreads before any coordinated response reaches the scene.

Core challenge: LoRa mesh topology with fire localisation logic — the system must identify which stall the fire originated in, not just that fire exists somewhere.
The Innovation Bar

What passes. What doesn't.

Judges evaluate against a clear standard. Know it before you submit.

✓ What Passes
✓ You can name at least 20 specific people whose lives are measurably worse because this device doesn't exist
✓ There is no affordable existing solution to this problem in Nigeria
✓ Your device produces a physical, observable output — not just data on a screen
✓ The problem came before the device — you didn't start with a component and build backwards
✕ What Fails
✕ The "innovation" is adding a microcontroller to something that already works fine without one
✕ The problem it solves is a first-world convenience, not a Nigerian necessity
✕ The device is primarily a demonstration of a technology rather than a solution to a problem
✕ Removing the electronics from the prototype would leave something that still mostly works
The Core Test — Ask Yourself
"If this device was given to its target user tomorrow, what specific thing would be different about their life in 30 days?"
Who Can Compete

Eligibility & Rules

Open wider than it's ever been. Any UNIBEN student. Any faculty. Any problem worth solving.

🎓
Any UNIBEN Faculty

All currently enrolled UNIBEN students are eligible regardless of faculty or department. A medical student who understands a health problem, paired with an engineer who can build the solution, is stronger than four engineers guessing at what patients need.

👥
Team Size

Compete individually or in teams of up to four members. A team leader must be designated as the primary point of contact. Mixed-faculty teams are encouraged and eligible for a dedicated recognition award.

🔩
Hardware Required

This is a hardware challenge. Software-only, app-only, or purely theoretical submissions are not eligible. The deliverable must be a physical device or system that produces observable output.

📊
Problem Must Be Real

Projects must address a demonstrable problem that affects real people in a measurable way. Decorative, aesthetic, or novelty devices are not eligible. Vague problem statements without evidence will not pass.

💡
Original Work Only

All projects must be original and not previously submitted to any other competition. Teams self-fund their hardware builds — cash prizes are awarded at the Grand Finale.

🗺
Domain Declaration

Each team must declare their thematic domain at registration. Multiple teams can work in the same domain — submissions are evaluated independently. Near-identical submissions will be investigated.

⚠ Automatic Disqualification

  • No quantified problem statement — "many people face this" without evidence
  • Solutions to problems that do not meaningfully affect Nigerians
  • Commercial products that already exist affordably in Nigeria
  • Software-only or app-only submissions
  • Projects where the innovation is the aesthetic, not the function
  • Pre-built robotics kits assembled without significant original engineering contribution
How You Will Be Scored

Judging Framework

Judges are drawn equally from academia and industry. Phase 1 submissions are reviewed blindly — judges don't know which team submitted which document.
