What This Is

Engineering that solves real problems

The Daddy G.O Innovation Challenge is an annual engineering competition hosted by NUESA UNIBEN. In its 4th edition, this year introduces a mandatory Design Phase before any hardware is built — because a device nobody needed is not an innovation.

Open to all UNIBEN students across all faculties. Mixed-faculty teams are explicitly encouraged and separately recognised.

How The Innovation Challenge Works

Three phases from idea to Grand Finale

No team builds anything before their problem has been evaluated. That's the rule. That's why this edition is different.

1
June 1 – June 14, 2026
Design Phase Submission

Prove the problem. Plan the solution. Pass the innovation gate. Submit a written design document before any hardware is built. Judges review blindly.

  • Problem Statement — real numbers, real people, real harm
  • Solution Overview — what the device does, exactly
  • Hardware Justification — reasoned component selection
  • Software/Logic Design — including error handling
  • Preliminary Component Budget — Nigerian supplier prices
  • Innovation Statement — why this needs to exist
2
June 23 – July 2, 2026
Build & Mentorship Week

Shortlisted teams are assigned mentors based on their specific design. Build your prototype, document the process, submit your video by July 2.

  • Assigned technical mentor matched to your domain
  • Working prototype (full or functional partial)
  • Build documentation — what changed and why
  • 2–3 minute video demonstration
3
July 10, 2026 · Grand Finale
Demonstrate. Defend. Win.

Present live to judges from academia and industry. Explain decisions, defend failures, prove impact. 8 minutes to present, 7 minutes of Q&A.

  • 8-minute live demonstration and presentation
  • 7-minute judges' Q&A
  • Judges' deliberation and scoring
  • Award ceremony — ₦1,000,000 first prize

Seeded Problem Directions

Need a starting point?

Examples of the calibre this challenge seeks. Teams aren't required to choose from this list — but every submission must meet this standard.

Health & Safety
Generator CO Poisoning Interceptor

CO from indoor generators kills hundreds annually. Cheap detectors beep — victims lose consciousness before they can act.

Core challenge: Relay cutoff on generator ignition + GSM alert in a battery-backed enclosure that works when mains power is out.
Water & Environment
Flood Early Warning Network

Benin City floods annually with no localised warning. Residents receive zero advance notice.

Core challenge: LoRa mesh with ultrasonic sensors at drainage chokepoints. No internet required. Solar-powered, 72-hour battery backup.
Commerce & Trade
Adulterated Fuel Detector

Adulterated petrol destroys engines. Industrial testers cost tens of thousands of dollars. No affordable option exists in Nigeria.

Core challenge: Capacitive/optical sensing of fuel dielectric properties. Pass/fail result before you refuel.
Agriculture & Food Security
Post-Harvest Cold Shelf

Market traders have no cold storage. End-of-day loss selling is the norm — not the exception.

Core challenge: PCM thermal architecture — maintain temperature 8–10 hours with zero active power during market hours.
Health & Safety
Anti-Sleep Alert for Drivers

Commercial driver fatigue causes mass-casualty accidents. No affordable embedded intervention exists.

Core challenge: Infrared eye-tracking + gyroscope, haptic + audible alarm. Self-calibrating to driver's baseline in darkness and vibration.
Health & Safety · Commerce
Smart Market Fire Alert Mesh

Market fires are recurrent. Poor communication means spread occurs before any coordinated response.

Core challenge: LoRa mesh with fire localisation — must identify which stall the fire originated in, not just that fire exists.
Who Can Compete

Eligibility & Rules

Open wider than ever. Any UNIBEN student. Any faculty. Any problem worth solving.

🎓
Any UNIBEN Faculty

All enrolled UNIBEN students regardless of faculty. A medical student paired with an engineer produces stronger innovation than four engineers guessing at what patients need.

👥
Team Size

Compete individually or in teams of up to four. A team leader must be designated. Mixed-faculty teams are eligible for a dedicated recognition award.

🔩
Hardware Required

This is a hardware challenge. Software-only, app-only, or theoretical submissions are not eligible. The deliverable must be a physical device.

📊
Problem Must Be Real

Projects must address a demonstrable problem affecting real people measurably. Decorative, novelty devices are not eligible.

💡
Original Work Only

Projects must not have been submitted to any other competition. Teams self-fund hardware — prizes awarded at the Grand Finale.

🗺
Domain Declaration

Declare your domain at submission. Multiple teams can work in the same domain — evaluated independently.

⚠ Automatic Disqualification

  • No quantified problem statement — "many people face this" without evidence
  • Solutions to problems that don't meaningfully affect Nigerians
  • Commercial products that already exist affordably in Nigeria
  • Software-only or app-only submissions
  • Projects where the innovation is aesthetic, not functional
  • Pre-built robotics kits without significant original engineering
